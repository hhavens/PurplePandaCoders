Privacy Policy.

How information and media may be handled when using Audio/Video Converter.

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Before publication: This is a draft policy. Confirm the app’s actual analytics, network access, downloads, logging, crash reporting, and support practices before treating it as your final privacy notice.

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Purple Panda Coders handles information in connection with the Audio/Video Converter macOS application and its product website. The practices described here should be checked against the released app and website configuration.

2. Files you choose to process

Audio/Video Converter is intended to process media files selected by the user. Media conversion using local tools such as FFmpeg can take place on your Mac. Whether files are transmitted elsewhere depends on the features you use and the final app implementation; this policy does not promise that every feature operates offline.

3. Online features and third-party services

If you use a feature that retrieves media or metadata from an online source, the app may connect to that source or its supporting services. These connections can disclose technical information such as your IP address and request details to the destination service. Online services have their own terms and privacy practices. Availability and behavior depend on the website or service involved.

4. Information collected by the developer

The developer may receive information that you voluntarily provide when requesting support, such as your email address, a description of an issue, or diagnostic material you choose to share. The website hosting provider may process routine technical access logs. Any analytics, telemetry, crash-reporting, or other collection used in the released product should be specifically disclosed here before publication.

5. Storage, retention, and security

Locally created output files and preferences may remain on your Mac until you remove them. Information received through support requests or website infrastructure may be retained as needed to respond, operate services, or meet legal obligations. Specific retention periods and security measures should be confirmed before launch.

6. Your choices

You can choose which media to process, remove local output files, and decide what information to include in support communications. You can contact the developer about personal information you have submitted directly.

7. Changes

This policy may be updated when features, service providers, or practices change. The updated version will be posted on this page with a revised date.

8. Contact

For privacy questions, visit the Purple Panda Coders website. Add a dedicated privacy contact address here if one is available.

Comic Zone privacy

Your privacy matters.

Learn how Comic Zone and the ComicHub community handle your information.

Last updated: September 3, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Comic Zone ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use the Comic Zone macOS application and the accompanying ComicHub forum.

01Information We Collect

  • Account information: If you create a forum account, we collect your email address, display name, and password (handled securely via Firebase Authentication).
  • Forum content: Posts, topics, and comments you submit to the ComicHub forum are stored and visible to other users.
  • Comic library data: Your comic files, folders, and reading metadata are stored locally on your device and are never uploaded to our servers.
  • Comic Vine metadata: When you use the Comic Vine scraper, Comic Zone sends comic titles or identifiers to the Comic Vine API to retrieve cover art and metadata. No personal information is included in these requests.

02How We Use Information

  • To create and manage your forum account
  • To display your posts and activity within the forum
  • To organize and enrich your local comic library with metadata
  • To respond to support requests

03Data Storage & Security

Forum accounts and content are stored using Firebase (Google Cloud). Your comic files and library organization stay on your own device unless you choose to back them up yourself. We do not sell your information to third parties.

04Third-Party Services

  • Firebase (Google) — authentication, forum data, and hosting
  • Comic Vine API — comic metadata and cover art

05Children's Privacy

Comic Zone is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

06Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your forum account information at any time by contacting us below.

07Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at support@purplepandacoders.com.

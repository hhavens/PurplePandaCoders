Guide

Comic Zone the app is still coming soon, but the ComicHub forum is live now. Here's how to get around it.

Creating an Account

  • Click Sign Up in the top right of the forum to register with an email address and password.
  • Already have an account? Click Sign In instead.
  • Once signed in, you'll see options to start topics and a Log out button in place of Sign In/Sign Up.

Browsing Categories

The forum home page lists discussion categories as cards. Click a category card to filter the topic list down to just that category.

Searching Discussions

Use the search box at the top of the forum to find topics by keyword.

Starting a Topic

  • Sign in, then click New Topic.
  • Choose a category, give your topic a title, and write your post.
  • Click Post Topic to publish it to the forum.

Replying to a Topic

Open any topic from the list to read it and add your own reply underneath.

Your Profile

Click your avatar or name to view your profile, or browse the member list to see other users' profiles.

Need Help?

Questions about the forum or Comic Zone? Reach out at support@purplepandacoders.com.
